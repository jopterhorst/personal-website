# Version 06 — Jop’s notebook

A separate comparison page at `/version-6/`. All five earlier versions remain unchanged.

A tactile personal notebook on a forest-green desk: paper texture, ring binding, index tabs, a taped portrait, handwritten annotations, a sticky note and lightly sketched project marks. Caveat, Lora and Courier Prime contrast handwriting, prose and annotations. Short personal copy focuses on practical business problems, automation and applying the same approach at church.

The portrait can turn over to reveal a brief personal note, with keyboard access and accessible state. The five public projects and their links stay visible. Native career disclosures retain the corrected current title and grouped Siemens roles. The page works without JavaScript; only the photo toggle and current-year update are enhanced. Responsive layouts keep a single reading order, with reduced-motion support. No build step or runtime dependencies; Google Fonts have local fallbacks.

All earlier designs are linked below the notebook. Marked `noindex,follow` for comparison. Hosting configuration is unchanged and nothing is deployed.

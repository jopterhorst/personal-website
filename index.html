WHAT KEEPS ME BUILDING

Same curiosity.
New possibilities.

I like getting to know a business: how people work, where they get stuck, and what could make life easier. From development and consulting to architecture and leadership, I’ve kept coming back to the same question: how do we turn this problem into something that works?

AI has changed the pace of building for me. Ideas that used to wait for time or capacity can become something I can try much sooner. I can explore unfamiliar tools and languages, with years of experience shaping the questions and decisions. That freedom makes me want to build even more.

FROM IDEAS TO ENTERPRISE REALITY

An idea is a start.
It has to hold up.

Building quickly is exciting. Running software in an enterprise adds other questions: who can access the data, how systems connect, who maintains it, and how it stays reliable as needs change.

That’s why my experience with Mendix matters to me. An enterprise application platform gives teams a foundation for architecture, integration, governance and the application lifecycle.

Siemens’ Intelligence Center X brings together Mendix for applications and orchestration, Graph Studio for enterprise knowledge and context, and AI Studio for AI and machine learning. I’m interested in how that connects faster experimentation to governed business workflows. Engineering judgment and responsible operation remain part of the work.
